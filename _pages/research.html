    About this map

    Each point represents a publication. The map is a two-dimensional PCA projection of genuine pretrained MiniLM embeddings of publication titles and abstracts. Proximity is approximate: connections reflect cosine similarities in the original embedding space, not distances in this map. Filtering never moves the points.

    Colors show four research themes I defined, not clusters discovered by the model. Related publications are the three highest-ranked papers within my collection, across all themes and years. These relative rankings do not necessarily indicate strong relationships. Similarity describes textual relatedness, not research quality or influence.
