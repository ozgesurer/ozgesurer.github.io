    About this map

    The map uses genuine pretrained MiniLM text embeddings and the original PCA coordinates from the publication analysis. Filtering never moves the points. The closest publications are ranked using cosine similarity in the full embedding space, across all four themes, rather than distance on this map. Similarity describes textual relatedness, not research quality or influence.
